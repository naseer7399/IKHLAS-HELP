1. What is this software?

Think of it as a digital school register + fee book + cash book that lives on a phone or computer, so nobody has to search through paper files.

Before (paper)

Admission register, fee register, receipt book and cash book. Hard to search, easy to lose, and the totals must be added by hand.

Now (this software)

Type a name or admission number and the student appears. Payments are added, balances are worked out automatically, and receipts print in one click.

For parents

A separate small app where parents see only their own child's fees, payments and school notices.

The three parts of the system

Why the cloud matters: without it, every phone or computer keeps its own separate copy, so a payment entered on the office computer would not appear on the principal's phone. With cloud sync on (it is switched on for our school), everyone sees the same live data. The sidebar shows a green Cloud sync on badge when it is working.

Works on a phone, like an app

Open the school link in Chrome on an Android phone, tap the menu (⋮) and choose Install app. The school's blue-and-gold IS icon is added to the home screen and the software opens full-screen, like any other app.

3. For Teachers

Your job in the software is simple: add new students correctly, and look up a child's details when you need them.

Adding a new student

Every student has…

  • A unique Admission Number (like an ID — never repeated)
  • Name, class, date of birth, phone, address
  • Father's and mother's names
  • Aadhaar numbers (optional, must be exactly 12 digits)

Tips for teachers

  • Type just a few letters of a name or number in the search box to find a child.
  • Always enter the date of birth. Parents need it to log into the Parent Portal.
  • Made a mistake? Tell Management — only they can edit or delete a student.

Class list

In Reports you can see a class-wise list of students and press Print. The printout has the school logo and name at the top, ready to put on the notice board or in a file.

4. For the Fee Collector

You use the Fee Payments door. You see two tabs only: Students and Payments.

Taking a fee from a parent

What the software does for you

  • Subtracts the discount and shows the net fee.
  • Adds every payment already made.
  • Shows the balance still due instantly.
  • Marks the fee Pending, Partial or Paid.

About WhatsApp & SMS

After you save a payment, the software asks if you want to message the parent. If you say yes, WhatsApp (or the SMS app) opens with the message already typed. You must still press the Send button yourself. This costs nothing, but it is not automatic.

The student needs a phone number on file for this to work.

Made a wrong entry? Fee collectors cannot edit or delete a payment (this protects the money records). Tell Management immediately — they can correct it, and the software will record an alert that a payment was changed.

6. Management alerts: knowing what changed

Management does not have to check every screen. The software keeps a diary of changes and tells you when something important happens.

What gets recorded

🧑‍🎓 Student enrolled 🧾 Fees billed 💵 Fees collected ⏰ Pending balance (every 2 weeks) 🗑 Fee deleted 🗑 Payment deleted 🗑 Student deleted

Open the ⚙ gear next to Recent activity to switch any type on or off. There is also a switch for device pop-ups; your phone will ask for permission the first time.

Please understand: pop-ups appear while the software is open (or installed and running in the background). They are not like normal phone notifications that can wake a fully closed app. If you have not opened the software for a while, the diary is still there waiting for you.

7. The Parent Portal

A separate, simple app just for parents. It lets them check fees and read school messages without coming to the office.

How a parent logs in

What parents CAN see

  • Their child's name, class, DOB, parents' names, phone, address
  • Each fee: net amount, paid, balance, and Pending / Partial / Paid
  • Full list of payments made
  • Notices: fee reminders and school announcements

What parents can NOT see

  • Aadhaar numbers (never sent to the Parent Portal)
  • Any other student
  • Accounts, expenses or school finances
  • They cannot change anything — it is read-only

Sending messages to parents

Fee reminders only reach the right people. When you choose "all" or a class, the software automatically leaves out parents who have already paid in full.

10. Staying safe

This software holds children's personal details, including Aadhaar numbers. A few habits keep them safe.

Do

  • Change the starting passwords in Settings.
  • Log out when you leave a shared computer or phone.
  • Share the school sign-in only with staff who need it.
  • Keep the monthly backup file somewhere safe.

Don't

  • Don't tell a parent the staff password.
  • Don't send Aadhaar numbers on WhatsApp.
  • Don't use "Reset to example data" — it replaces the school's records. It needs a separate Data password for this reason.
  • Don't give the Parent Portal login to anyone but that child's parent.

Two sign-in steps

Honest limits: the role passwords inside the software are a convenience for a small staff team — they are not bank-level security. The Parent Portal uses Admission No. + date of birth, which is easy for parents but could be guessed by someone who knows both. Treat admission numbers and birth dates as private.

11. Common questions

I entered a payment on my phone — will Management see it on their computer?

Yes, as long as the green "Cloud sync on" badge is showing. It usually appears within moments, with no refresh needed.

The internet is slow in the village. Can I still use it?

Once the software has been opened once, the app screens load from your device. Data changes need the internet to reach the shared cloud, so if the signal is poor, wait for it to come back before relying on the sync badge.

A parent says they cannot log into the Parent Portal.

Check the Admission No. and date of birth on the student's profile page. If the date of birth is missing or wrong, Management should correct it, then the parent can try again.

I pressed "Send WhatsApp" but the parent did not get it.

The software only opens WhatsApp with the message ready. You must press Send in WhatsApp. Also check the phone number saved for the student.

We lost or changed a phone / computer. Is the data gone?

No. With cloud sync the data is stored online, and Management also keeps monthly backup files. Sign in on the new device and everything appears.

Can a teacher see fees?

No. The Fees, Payments and Accounts tabs are hidden for teachers.

Who do I ask for help?

Ask the school Management first. They can contact the person who set up the software if something needs fixing.
